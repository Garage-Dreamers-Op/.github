# Security policy

Garage-Dreamers-Op builds smart lockers: hardware that guards physical access. We take reports about their security seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@REPLACE-ME.example** with:

- what you found and where (product, device or locker, software version if known),
- the steps to reproduce it,
- the impact you expect.

Please report privately: don't open a public issue, and give us time to fix the problem before you disclose it.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within 5 business days.
- We keep you informed while we investigate and fix it.
- We credit you when the fix is released, unless you prefer otherwise.

We won't pursue legal action against good-faith research that respects this policy, avoids harming people, property and data, and stops at what is needed to demonstrate the problem.
